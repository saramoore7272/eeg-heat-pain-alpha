Figure 1. Recording retention and EEG screening.
(A) The locked screen retained 31 of 59 rated-heat recordings for the primary model. Of the 28 noncontributing recordings, 15 had no block with at least five passing seconds and 13 had a passing block without a qualifying repeat pair. A pair required at least five common seconds at the same programmed heat level and within 0.10°C. (B) Each point is the percentage of the 90 assessed seconds per recording passing the indicated screen. Vertical marks show group medians. The scalp screen used the 0.5–150 µV peak-to-peak criterion after prespecified channel omissions. The eye screen required both filtered eye leads to remain below 100 µV peak to peak. Panels show the 59 confirmation recordings from OpenNeuro ds008349 v2.0.1, using 25–35 s after each block marker.

Figure 2. Locked C3/C4 alpha result.
(A) Each point is a matched block pair. Later-minus-earlier differences in ratings and natural-log alpha power were residualized against measured temperature differences and the heat-level-specific repeat contrast. Alpha power (8–13 Hz) was obtained from fixed local C3 and C4 references. The fitted slope gives each participant equal total weight. (B) The adjusted estimate was +1.62% alpha power per 10 released rating units (95% participant-bootstrap confidence interval −1.44 to +4.21%, two-sided p = 0.2624; 31 participants and 140 pairs). The interval used 9,999 resamples of whole participants. OpenNeuro ds008349 v2.0.1, rated heat, 25–35 s after each block marker.

Figure 3. Sensitivity to eye-artifact correction.
Points and bars are adjusted estimates and 95% participant-bootstrap confidence intervals from the same model. Slow eye-channel regression reduced median eye-aligned frontal EEG RMS from 12.2 to 2.0 µV in separate validation windows across 56 validated recordings. Original passing seconds kept their original alpha values. Correction recovered matched pairs in 19 of 28 originally excluded participants. The expanded analysis estimated +0.13% per 10 rating units (95% CI −1.15 to +1.35%, two-sided p = 0.7750; 50 participants, 312 pairs). The corrected analysis restricted to the original participants estimated −0.57% (95% CI −1.95 to +0.97%; 31 participants, 227 pairs). Correction was evaluated as a post hoc sensitivity check in the same cohort. OpenNeuro ds008349 v2.0.1, rated heat, 25–35 s after each block marker.
